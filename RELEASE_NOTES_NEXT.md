# Release notes — next release (draft)

_Draft for the next release after 1.8.0. Keep adding to this as more changes land._

## ✨ General Improvements

- **New tool: Stamp** (⌘5). Add a small text overlay — "Example School Band", "Property of the Music Dept" — to your parts, like a rubber stamp. Set the text — with **bold and italic on just the words you choose** (⌘B / ⌘I, or the Style buttons) — plus font, size, colour and an optional box around it, then put it anywhere on the page: click one of the nine quick positions, or just **drag the stamp around on the preview**. Save as many named stamps as you like.
- The Stamp tab also stamps PDFs you already have: add them, choose every page or just the first page of each, and either **replace the originals** or save copies under names you type.
- Stamps are burned into the page, so they print everywhere and can't be deleted out of the PDF.
